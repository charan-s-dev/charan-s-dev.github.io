MyInvois SME Booster

WhatsApp e-invoicing for Malaysian SMEs

Rosemallow Technologies · developer of the WhatsApp bot and its FastAPI backend

Problem

Filling and validating up to 55 fields per e-invoice on LHDN's MyInvois portal takes about 10 minutes at a desk; in recorded demos the bot cuts that to 1–2 minutes from a phone.

Features

CineAd

Cloud ad publishing for standalone theatres

Rosemallow Technologies · sole developer of the kiosk and controller app

Problem

Theatre ads still travel by pen drive, email, and WhatsApp and are loaded by hand at each screen, so a campaign across 100 theatres is hard to update, track, or prove it ran.

Features

One app runs as a theatre kiosk or an operator's remote: the kiosk pairs to its screen with a one-time token and plays ads at showtime; the remote finds it by mDNS to pause, skip, or restart.

Engineering

Runs a WebSocket control server with heartbeats that drop vanished phones, auto-skips a clip that hangs for 6 seconds, and shows a neutral slate on the cinema screen instead of any error.
